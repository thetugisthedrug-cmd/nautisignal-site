How It Works

Report. Verify. Confirm.

A simple, reliable workflow for managing catch reports, confirming key details, and keeping the whole fleet informed — by voice, app, or text.

01Report The boat calls it in

By radio, app, or text — the moment the fish is released.

VOICE · APP · SMS Where it goesThe call goes out on the tournament voice channel — every radio in the fleet and the director’s console hear it as it is spoken.
02Capture Every detail is logged

Vessel identity, server timestamp, GPS position, species, angler.

BOAT 23 · 10:42:07 · GPS FIX Where it goesThe report lands in the director’s live queue the moment it arrives, with the boat’s GPS fix and an official server timestamp attached automatically.
03Verify Checked against the rules

Boundary, fishing hours, and boat identity validated for review.

IN BOUNDS · LINES IN Where it goesThe checks run automatically and the results sit beside the catch on the director’s screen — ready for review, nothing retyped.

FAQ

Questions directors ask first.

Is NautiSignal meant to replace VHF radio?

No. NautiSignal supports your tournament communication workflow — organizing reports, verifying details, and broadcasting confirmations — alongside the radio you already trust.

Are handhelds required?

No. NautiSignal is an all-inclusive tournament service. Assigned handhelds can be included when needed, and specialty gear like satellite or Starlink backup can be added only if your tournament requires it.

Can captains use a mobile app instead?

Yes. Mobile access works as a backup option or as part of a lighter tournament setup.

How is pricing set?

NautiSignal is priced per boat across three levels — Base, Pro, and Full Signal — with volume pricing for larger fields. Because every event differs in size, length, and needs, we build a final quote around your tournament.

How long does setup take?

We handle it. Radios arrive provisioned and paired to your boat list, and your tournament — confirmation codes, boundary, fishing hours, and the voice channel — is configured and checked off a launch checklist before day one.

What happens if a boat loses coverage offshore?

Radios carry both cellular and Wi-Fi and switch between them automatically. The live voice channel keeps working wherever there is signal, and catches can always be called in the moment coverage returns — nothing is lost in between. Satellite backup can be added for events that run far offshore.

How are catches verified?

Every report carries the boat's identity, a server timestamp, the reported species and angler, and a GPS boundary check. Confirmations use unique color-and-number codes, and each one is logged with a delivery receipt and the boat's acknowledgment — a complete record for any protest.

Do spectators need an app to follow along?

No. The live page works in any browser from a link or the QR code on the dock — no login, no download. There's a big-screen feed for TVs at the marina, and the live audio broadcast means the crowd hears every catch as it happens.

What do we get for scoring?

A one-click export of every catch in receive order with the official tie-break sequence. Each row carries its status and the director's reason for any disqualification, with a single counts-for-scoring column so a disqualified catch can never be scored by mistake.

Who runs NautiSignal on event day?

Your tournament director, from one dashboard — and clean reports can confirm automatically if you want them to. Full Signal events include on-site setup and support, so the technology is our job while the tournament stays yours.
