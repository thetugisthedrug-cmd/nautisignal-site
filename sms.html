SMS Policy

NautiSignal

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This page describes NautiSignal’s text messaging (SMS) program: what messages we send, how consent is collected, and how to opt out. It supplements our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

1. What messages we send

NautiSignal sends two kinds of text messages, both tied to operating offshore fishing tournaments:

  • Account verification codes — one-time setup and sign-in codes used to verify your identity and activate the NautiSignal captain application.
  • Tournament operations messages — messages related to a tournament you are participating in, such as catch confirmations and schedule updates.

We do not send promotional or marketing text messages. The NautiSignal SMS program is strictly limited to the two transactional message types above. It is never used for marketing, promotions, advertising, sweepstakes, raffles, giveaways, contests, prizes, polls, or sponsorship, and it never carries content from, or sent on behalf of, any third party. Audience-engagement features such as trivia, raffles, giveaways, live polls, shout-outs, and sponsor moments are part of NautiSignal’s live audio broadcast — they are delivered by voice to the tournament audience and are never sent by text message.

2. How consent is collected

Mobile phone numbers are collected on the NautiSignal captain setup form, which participants complete when registering a vessel for a tournament. The form is publicly viewable at:

https://app.nautisignal.com/captain/setup

A sample of the SMS consent step, showing exactly how it appears on that form, is published at nautisignal.com/sms-optin.html.

Consent to receive text messages is given by checking a separate, unchecked-by-default checkbox displayed next to the mobile phone field. The checkbox presents the following wording:

“I agree to receive text messages from NautiSignal at the mobile number provided, including account verification codes and tournament operations messages such as catch confirmations and schedule updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our Privacy Policy and SMS Policy.”

The form cannot be submitted without this box being checked, and the date and time of consent are recorded with the submission.

3. Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies based on your activity — for example, how often you sign in and how many catches your vessel reports during a tournament. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier’s plan.

4. Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP to any NautiSignal message to opt out of SMS at any time.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at info@nautisignal.com.

Opting out of SMS does not affect your tournament participation, but one-time passcodes cannot be delivered by text after opting out; contact us for alternative sign-in arrangements.

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information shared with subprocessors solely to deliver the messaging service (for example, our SMS delivery provider) is limited to what is required to send the messages and is excluded from any such sharing.

5. Consent is not a condition of purchase

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any NautiSignal product or service.

6. Other forms on this website

The contact and quote request forms on this website include an optional phone field. Numbers provided there are used only to respond to your inquiry by voice call and are never enrolled in the SMS program described on this page.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: info@nautisignal.com.
